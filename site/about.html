My Story

From Jamaica to Building a Global Remote Business

I started with no connections, no fancy equipment, and no roadmap — just a laptop and a decision to figure out how remote work actually operates.

Growing up in Jamaica, the idea of working with clients on the other side of the world felt distant. Learning the skills was one part of it; learning how to position them, price them, and find the right clients was another.

I started with lower-paying remote opportunities while I built real, marketable skills. As my skills grew, so did the quality of the clients I worked with and the rates I could charge. Over time, that work became a virtual-assistance company — one that now generates $12K+ in monthly revenue and works with clients internationally.

My journey taught me that where you start does not have to determine how far your skills can travel.

None of this was about getting rich quickly. It was about building, one client and one skill at a time, toward a life with more choice — in how I earn, where I live, and how present I get to be for the people and experiences that matter.

That's what I want for you too: not just a remote job, but a way of building a life that travels with you.
